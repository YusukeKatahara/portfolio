A branch of the vagus nerve runs close to the surface of the ear. **taVNS (transcutaneous auricular Vagus Nerve Stimulation)** activates the vagus nerve by applying a weak electrical stimulus through the skin there. Unlike conventional vagus nerve stimulation, which requires surgically implanted electrodes, taVNS can engage the autonomic nervous system non-invasively — which is why it is being studied worldwide.

My research asks **how far we can quantify its effects on autonomic and circulatory dynamics using wearable biosignals such as PPG (photoplethysmography)**. If indices that used to require large laboratory equipment can be reproduced with everyday wearable sensors, it builds a major bridge for both research and application.

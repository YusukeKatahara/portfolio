**V EFFECT** is an SNS app for recording daily effort and building habits by sharing them with peers. It is designed around one effect: *things you can't keep up alone become sustainable when someone is watching* — and it tries to maximize that.

Planning, UI design, implementation, App Store review, and post-launch operation are all done **by one person**. The stack is Flutter + Firebase, and the app is live on the App Store.

## What I learned

A product isn't "done when built" — its real substance appears only in operation. Handling store reviews, seeing how users actually behave, hitting retention walls: everything learned here feeds directly back into how I design research projects.
